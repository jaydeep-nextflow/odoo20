Odoo Module · Point Of Sale Employee Attendance

Point Of Sale
Employee Attendance

This module enables employees to conveniently check in and check out of work directly through the system, ensuring accurate attendance tracking and workforce management. Employees can record their arrival and departure times with a simple and user-friendly interface.

✔ Employee Login & Authentication

Allow employees to securely log in and log out directly from the POS interface using role-based access controls.

✔ Smart Check-In & Check-Out

Enable employees to quickly record attendance with a simple check-in/check-out process from the POS screen.
