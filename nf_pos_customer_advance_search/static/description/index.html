Customer Advance Search in POS

The POS customers Quick Search & Selection module enhances the standard Odoo Point of Sale customer selection process by providing a fast and convenient way for customers to search and assign customers directly from the POS interface.

The POS customer Quick Search & Selection module simplifies the default Odoo POS customer workflow by replacing the need to open a separate customer screen with a smart dropdown-based search system directly inside the POS interface. customers can quickly search and assign customers without interrupting the billing process, making checkout faster and more efficient.

The system allows customers to be searched instantly using their name or mobile number, with real-time filtering that updates results as the customer types. The dropdown also displays key customer details such as name and mobile number, helping users easily identify the correct customer even when multiple records have similar names.

A dedicated search button further enhances usability by allowing instant filtering of the customer list, improving lookup speed during high-volume sales operations. Overall, this module streamlines customer selection in POS, reduces manual effort, and significantly improves customer productivity during daily transactions.
