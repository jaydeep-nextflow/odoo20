POS Dashboard

Point of Sale Dashboard provides a powerful analytics dashboard for Odoo POS, enabling managers and business owners to monitor sales performance, analyze trends, and make informed business decisions. The dashboard offers real-time KPIs, interactive charts, top-performing products and customers, payment method analysis, and customizable date-wise reporting—all in one place.
With intuitive visualizations and comprehensive sales insights, this module helps improve operational efficiency and supports data-driven decision-making.

Everything you need to know in one video

Point of Sale Dashboard is a comprehensive analytics module for Odoo that provides real-time insights into your POS business performance. Monitor daily, monthly, and yearly sales with interactive dashboards, KPI cards, and dynamic charts. Track active POS sessions, total orders, total sales amount, products sold, average selling price, monthly revenue, today's top sales, highest monthly sales, top customers, top-selling products, and payment method distribution through visually appealing pie charts. Filter reports using custom start and end dates, export charts in SVG, PNG, and CSV formats, and make faster, data-driven business decisions with an intuitive dashboard fully integrated into Odoo Point of Sale. This module helps retailers, restaurants, supermarkets, and other businesses efficiently monitor sales performance, analyze customer behavior, identify best-selling products, and improve overall business profitability through powerful visual analytics and reporting.
