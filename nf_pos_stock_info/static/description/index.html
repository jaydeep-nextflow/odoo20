Point of Sale Display Stock

Point of Sale Display Stock helps POS cashiers instantly check product stock availability directly from the Point of Sale product screen.
Instead of switching back to the backend Inventory module, users can view the latest stock quantities while creating POS orders.

✔ Display Live Product Stock in POS

View available product stock directly on the POS product screen without opening the Inventory module.

✔ Configuration-Based Stock Display

Enable or disable stock quantity display from POS settings according to your business requirements.
