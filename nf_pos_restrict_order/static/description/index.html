Point Of Sale Restrict Out Of Stock

Point of Sale Restrict Out Of Stock helps businesses prevent selling products that are not available in inventory. The module automatically blocks cashiers from adding or validating products that are out of stock, ensuring that only available items can be sold through the Odoo Point of Sale.
By preventing out-of-stock sales in real time, businesses can maintain accurate inventory, eliminate overselling, reduce stock discrepancies, and improve customer satisfaction.

Why Choose This Module?

  • ✓ Managing inventory in a retail environment requires accurate stock control. Selling unavailable products leads to customer dissatisfaction, inventory inconsistencies, and additional operational work.

📊 Point Of Sale Restrict Out Of Stock workflow

Point Of Sale → Menu Click "Configuration" → Click "Settings" Enable Restrict Out Of Stock Order Creation

In POS orders, display the product stock status on the order line: green if the product is available, and red if it is out of stock.
