Pos Toppings

POS Toppings in Odoo lets you add customizable extras to your products directly in the Point of Sale. Customers can choose from optional or mandatory toppings, each with its own price, while the system updates totals and inventory automatically. Receipts clearly show the parent product with its selected toppings, making order management fast, accurate, and customer-friendly.

Platform Support

Community Enterprise Odoo.sh

The POS Toppings feature in Odoo allows restaurants, cafes, and retail businesses to offer customizable options for their products directly in the Point of Sale interface. It enables assigning one or more products as toppings to a parent product, giving customers flexibility to personalize their orders. Toppings can be optional or mandatory, can have their own prices, and are grouped for easy selection. The feature ensures that selected toppings are reflected in the order, automatically updating quantities, prices, and inventory. Receipts clearly display the parent product along with its toppings, providing transparency for both staff and customers. Designed for a fast and intuitive POS experience, this feature streamlines order customization while maintaining accurate pricing and inventory tracking.

Hot Features

Optional or Mandatory Toppings

Configure whether a topping is optional or must be selected before completing the order.

Price Control

Automatically update the total price based on selected toppings. Can be free or charged.

Quantity Management

Track the quantity of each topping added to the parent product in the POS interface.

POS Visibility

Toppings are directly selectable in the POS screen, making it fast for cashiers or waitstaff to add extras.

Receipt Clarity

Parent products and their toppings appear clearly on the receipt, showing each item and its price.

Category Grouping

Toppings can be grouped into categories for easier selection (e.g., sauces, cheese, extras).

Click on the products and navigate to the product variants.

Choose the product for which you want to add a topping product.

Now you will see the ‘Topping’ checkbox.

Now select the ‘Topping’ checkbox.

Click on the products and navigate to the product variants.

Now select another product and set it as a topping product.

Now select the ‘Topping’ checkbox.

Now choose another product from the product variants and navigate to the Toppings tab.

Create a Toppings Group, choose the product that you marked as a topping product, and navigate to the Point of Sale.

In the Point of Sale, open the register to start operations for the restaurant.

Choose the product that has a topping product.

Select your desired option and click the ‘Add’ button.

Now choose the topping product as you want.

Now remove the parent topping product.

Now you can see that the parent product with the topping product shows quantity and amount as 0, and the total amount is also 0.

Now, reselect the topping product and proceed to payment.

Now, click ‘Validate’ to confirm the payment.

On the generated receipt, you can see the parent product along with the topping product.
