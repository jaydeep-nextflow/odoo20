City Wise Delivery Charges | Website Shipping Method Charges Based on City

City Wise Delivery Charges extends Odoo's Sales, Website, and Delivery features by automatically applying shipping charges based on the customer's selected city.
Instead of using a fixed delivery fee for all customers, businesses can configure different delivery prices for each city, ensuring accurate logistics costing and a better customer experience.

✔ Automatic City-Based Delivery Charges

Automatically calculate and apply delivery charges based on the customer's selected city across Sales Orders, Website Checkout.

✔ Multi-City & Multi-Company Support

Configure separate delivery charges for multiple cities while allowing each company to maintain its own pricing structure independently.
