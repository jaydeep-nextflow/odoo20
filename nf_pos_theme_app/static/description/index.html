Explore the complete Odoo Point of Sale Theme module in action and discover how it transforms your POS interface with a modern design, improved usability, and a professional shopping experience.

The Odoo Point of Sale Theme module allows you to enhance the appearance of your Odoo POS with a clean, modern, and user-friendly interface. Once installed, the custom POS theme is automatically applied to the Point of Sale, providing an attractive layout, refined colors, improved buttons, and a well-organized screen for faster navigation. Designed to improve both usability and visual appeal, the theme helps cashiers work more efficiently while delivering a polished and consistent POS experience without modifying the core Point of Sale functionality.

Point of Sale Dashboard

The POS (Point of Sale) Dashboard module is a crucial feature designed to provide an overview of sales and operations in real time within a retail or hospitality environment. It integrates with the Odoo POS system, offering a user-friendly interface to track and manage sales data.

Tailor Order Management

The Tailor Management System in Odoo streamlines the entire tailoring process, from designing clothes to order management. It includes dynamic measurements, sale orders, invoicing, and manufacturing orders for efficient production. The system features chatter for communication, fabric selection, and custom design options. With built-in reporting and analytics, tailors can track sales, customer preferences, and workflow seamlessly.
