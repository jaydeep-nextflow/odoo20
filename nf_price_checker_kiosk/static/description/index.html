Price Checker Kiosk

The Price Checker Kiosk module provides a simple and efficient interface to quickly view product information in Odoo. Users can scan or enter product barcodes to instantly see details such as product name, price, stock quantity, and variants. The module supports both light and dark modes, ensuring a comfortable viewing experience in any environment. Display options are customizable, allowing the company logo and relevant product fields to be shown according to your needs.

Platform Support

Community Enterprise Odoo.sh

The module is designed for ease of use and security. Administrators can assign “Price Checker Employee” access to control who can use the kiosk while restricting configuration settings. Navigation is straightforward, making it ideal for touchscreen kiosks, tablets, or desktops. This module is perfect for quickly providing employees or customers with accurate product information without additional complexity.

install the Price Checker Kiosk module.

Go to Settings.

Click on the 'Price Checker Kiosk' setting.

Mark the options you want to display for the product details, click 'Save' to apply, and then click on 'Launch Price Checker Kiosk'

As shown in the image, you can click on “Light” to switch to the light view of the screen.

Enter the product’s default code to view its details, and click the “Moon” button to switch to dark mode.

Enter the product’s barcode to view its details.

Now uncheck all the product options and navigate to the Price Checker Kiosk.

As shown in the image, a warning message is displayed: “Please configure the product details as required.”

Now select the desired options and click on “Sales”.

Now click on 'Products', and then click on 'Product Variants'.

Now navigate to your desired product.

Copy the barcode of the product you want.

Now click on 'Settings'

Now navigate to the 'Price Checker Kiosk' settings and check the 'Display Company Logo' option.

Now click on 'Users & Companies' and navigate to 'Users'.

Now click on the user to whom you want to give access to the 'Price Checker Kiosk'.

Now navigate to the 'PRICE CHECKER' section and select 'Price Checker Kiosk' as 'Price Checker Employee'.

Now you can see that the employee can view the 'Price Checker' menu. Next, navigate to 'Settings'.

Now you can check that the employee cannot see the configuration.

Now click on the 'Price Checker' menu.

Now enter the barcode to view the product details.
