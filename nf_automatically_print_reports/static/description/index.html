Automatically Print Reports

The Automatically Print Reports module for Odoo enables seamless automation of PDF printing. With this module, any PDF report from standard Odoo modules can be printed automatically without manual intervention. Whether it’s invoices, sales orders, vendor bills, or any other PDF report, this tool ensures every document is generated and printed efficiently, saving time and reducing repetitive tasks.

This module is ideal for businesses looking to streamline their workflow and improve operational efficiency. By automating report printing across all modules, it reduces errors, increases productivity, and ensures that every critical document is printed on time. Perfect for companies that rely heavily on document management and want a fully automated printing solution in Odoo.

Navigate to the Settings

In the General Settings section, find the Automatically Print PDF Reports option, check the Automatically Print PDF Report checkbox, and click the Save button to apply the changes.

Navigate to the Invoice module

Now, click on 'Vendors' and then click on 'Bills'.

Now, select any bill, click on the 'Print' button, and then click on the 'Invoice PDF' button.

As you can see, the PDF has been printed.

Now, navigate to the 'Sales' module.

Click on any sales order to navigate to the sales order.

Now, click on the button that looks like a settings icon, then click on the 'Print' button, and finally click on the 'PDF Quote' button.

Now, the PDF has been printed. You can see that the PDF is the same as the one you selected.
