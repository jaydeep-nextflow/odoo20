Tailor Management System

The Tailor Management System in Odoo streamlines the entire tailoring process, from designing clothes to order management. It includes dynamic measurements, sale orders, invoicing, and manufacturing orders for efficient production. The system features chatter for communication, fabric selection, and custom design options. With built-in reporting and analytics, tailors can track sales, customer preferences, and workflow seamlessly.

Measurement Management

The Measurement Module helps in recording and managing customer measurements dynamically, ensuring accurate fitting for custom-made clothing.

  • Dynamic Measurement Types
  • Measurement Categories
  • Upper Body Measurements (Shirts, Jackets, Blouses)
  • Lower Body Measurements
  • Measurement History

Designing the Clothes

The Design master allows tailors and designers to create, customize, and finalize clothing designs based on customer preferences. It includes the following features:

  • Fabric & Material Selection
  • Custom Design Options
  • Style & Pattern Selection
  • Adding Notes for Designers

Customer & Measurement Management
  • Customer Profiles - Save customer details, order history, and preferences.
  • Dynamic Measurement System - Standard sizes, custom measurements.
  • Dynamic Measurement Categories - Upper body, lower body, full-body, and special custom measurements.
  • Customer Notes & Attachments - Add specific design notes, images, or sketches
Custom Design & Fabric Selection
  • Fabric Management - Store fabric types, stock levels, and prices.
  • Style Selection - Predefined design templates or fully customizable designs.
Order & Sale Management
  • Tailor Order Management - Create, update, and track orders.
  • Order ID Generation - Unique order tracking numbers for easy reference.
  • Order Status Workflow - Draft, Confirmed, Delivered.
Manufacturing & Production Management
  • Manufacturing Orders (MO) - One click MO from tailor orders.
  • Raw Material Management - Track fabric, thread, buttons, and other materials.
Invoicing & Payments
  • Invoice Generation - Linked to sales order and payment.
  • Multiple Payment Methods - Cash, Card, Bank Transfer.
  • Tax & Discount Management - Apply taxes and promotional discounts.
Delivery & Pickup Management
  • Order Pickup Scheduling - Customers can select pickup time.
  • Delivery Confirmation System - Update status once delivered.
Reporting & Analytics
  • Sales Reports - Sale order quotation report.
  • Tailor Performance Reports - Detaild report of tailor order.
