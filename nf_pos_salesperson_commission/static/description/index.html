Pos Salesperson Commission

The Pos Salesperson Commission module in Odoo provides a comprehensive and flexible solution for managing sales-based incentives. It supports both percentage-based and fixed commission structures, allowing businesses to define rules based on products or product variants for precise calculation. The module integrates seamlessly with the Point of Sale (POS), automatically generating commission records in real time as orders are validated. It also enables salesperson assignment at the order line level, ensuring accurate tracking of individual performance. With role-based access control, organizations can differentiate between Commission Managers and Commission Users, maintaining both flexibility and data security.

In addition to automation, the module offers a complete commission lifecycle, from creation to payment and expense generation. Commissions can be marked as paid and instantly converted into expense records, simplifying financial tracking and reducing manual work. The system ensures that users can only access their own records, while administrators retain full visibility for oversight and reporting. With its user-friendly interface, seamless backend and POS synchronization, and scalable design, this module enhances operational efficiency, minimizes errors, and provides clear, audit-friendly records—making it a powerful tool for managing salesperson incentives in any business environment.

What is the Salesperson Commission module used for?

Go to Settings.

To configure the salesperson commission in Odoo, navigate to Users & Companies, then click on Users.

Select the desired user from the list.

Navigate to the Salesperson Commission section, choose the POS Commission Management Rights, set it to Commission Manager, and then click Save.

Now go to the Point of Sale module.

Click Configuration, then click Settings.

Navigate to the Salesperson Commission section, enable Apply Commission, select the Commission Employee, and click Save.

Go to Commissions, click on Commission Rules, and configure the commission rules.

Click the New button to create a Commission Rule.

Set the Salespersons and Products, choose the Commission Type as Percentage, and enter the desired commission amount.

The commission rule Rule-00001 has been created. Now, navigate to the Point of Sale module.

Click on “Open Register” to start a new POS session.

Next, choose the product that was previously specified in the Commission Rule.

Now, click on the three dots (⋮).

Now, click on the Salesperson button.

Now, select the salesperson who was previously chosen in the Commission Rules.

At this point, the salesperson is assigned to the order line. Now, click on the Payment button.

Now, click on the Validate button to confirm the order.

Now, the salesperson is shown on the receipt. Next, go to the backend by clicking the Backend button.

Go to Commissions, click on Commissions.

Now, you can see that the salesperson commission has been created successfully. Next, click on the commission line that was recently created.

Now, you can see the details you selected: Salesperson, Product, and the Amount, which has been calculated based on the commission, commission type, and product price. Next, click on Commissions, then click on Commission Rules.

Click on the commission rule record that was previously created.

Now, set the Commission Type to Fixed, enter your desired commission amount, click the Save button to save the form, and then go to the Point of Sale module.

Click on “Continue Selling” to resume sales in the POS.

Next, choose the product that was previously specified in the Commission Rule.

Now, click on the three dots (⋮).

Now, click on the Salesperson button.

Now, select the salesperson who was previously chosen in the Commission Rules.

At this point, the salesperson is assigned to the order line. Now, click on the Payment button.

Next, choose another product variant that was previously specified in the Commission Rule.

After assigning the salesperson to all the order lines, click the Payment button to proceed with the payment.

Now, click on the Validate button to confirm the order.

Now, the salesperson is shown on the receipt. Next, go to the backend by clicking the Backend button.

Go to Commissions, click on Commissions.

Now, you can see that the commission has been created based on the POS order.
