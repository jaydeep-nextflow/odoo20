Product Multi Company

This module provides multi-company control for products and product variants in Odoo. It allows you to assign products to specific companies and manage access using the "Product Multi Company Access" feature. Based on the user’s role, especially for users set as "Multi Company Manager", the system controls actions like creating, updating, and mass updating product company assignments. It also enables users to switch between companies from the top-right company selector and ensures that product visibility changes according to the selected company.

Platform Support

Community Enterprise Odoo.sh

Another key feature of this module is access restriction and UI control. It prevents unauthorized users from performing sensitive actions, such as mass updating product company data, by showing an "Access Denied" message. It also hides the "Companies" field in the product and product variant forms based on access according to manager and user level, keeping the interface clean and secure. Overall, it ensures proper separation of data between companies and maintains strict multi-company management in Odoo.
