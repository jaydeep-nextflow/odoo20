Payment Provider: Hesabe

Payment Provider: Hesabe is an Odoo payment integration module that enables businesses to accept secure online payments through Hesabe, a trusted and widely used payment gateway.

The integration supports real-time payment processing, automatic transaction status updates, and smooth reconciliation within Odoo, making it ideal for eCommerce and online service payments.
